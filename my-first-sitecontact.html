Connect With SGA

Please contact our President, Chief of Staff, or any of our Executive Board members with questions, concerns, or suggestions. We are here to represent the student body and help improve the student experience at Mount St. Joseph University.

Meetings

SGA's general assembly meetings are held on the first Tuesday of the month at 4:00pm in the Student Organization Center (SOC). All students are welcome to attend and share their ideas, concerns, and suggestions with SGA.

Share Your Feedback

Please contact our President, Chief of Staff, or any Executive Board member with feedback. Feel free to attend our general assembly meetings, or set up individual meetings with SGA members to share your ideas.

Get Involved

There are many ways students can become involved with the Student Government Association. Please attend one of our general assembly meetings, connect with your class representative, share your ideas and concerns, or apply for an SGA leadership position.
