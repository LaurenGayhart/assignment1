Who We Are

The Mount St. Joseph University Student Government Association is a student organization designed to represent the interests, ideas, and concerns of students across campus.

Our Purpose

The Student Government Association represents and serves the interests of the student body at Mount St. Joseph University. SGA strives to help students understand their rights, privileges, and responsibilities as students at Mount St. Joseph University. SGA works in accordance with and in respect for the Mission of the University in all endeavors.

What We Do

Student Representation

SGA provides students with an opportunity to communicate their ideas, concerns, and suggestions about the university.

Campus Partnerships

All executive members of SGA report to MSJ faculty committees, where we share students ideas and opinions directly with high-level university administrators. SGA also works with faculty and staff to help implement student ideas and concerns. We also fund all SGA-recgnized student organizations and help promote student events and activities across campus.

Student Organizations

SGA supports recognized student organizations and helps promote opportunities for students to become involved on campus. Any organization ho is interested in becoming recognized can submit an application to SGA for review and approval.
