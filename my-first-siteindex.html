Welcome to SGA

Welcome to the Mount St. Joseph University Student Government Association. This website will provide students with information about SGA, its leadership, upcoming meetings, campus initiatives, and opportunities to get involved.

Our Mission

The Student Government Association works to promote the general welfare of students by creating partnerships with faculty, staff, and employees while providing students with a voice in campus policies and decisions.

Get Involved

There are many ways students can become involved with the Student Government Association. Here are some ways to get started:

  • Attend SGA meetings
  • Connect with your class representative
  • Share ideas and concerns
  • Apply for an SGA leadership position

Student Voice

SGA serves as a voice for all students. We strive to implement student ideas and concerns to improve the student experience at Mount St. Joseph University.
